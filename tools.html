Study tools

Fourteen exercises that run in your browser: no sign-up, no data transfer, no storage. Every tool starts with the exercise, explanations follow below. The tools are in German.

Languages

For English and French at German upper secondary school. Each language has four tools: grammar, text analysis, writing and the speaking exam.

English

English grammar (Englisch-Trainer)

Tenses, conditionals, reported speech, the passive and typical mistakes of German learners as flashcards, plus connectors and a quiz.

From Englisch Meistern

English text analysis (Textanalyse)

Task verbs by competence level and the key stylistic devices as flashcards, with a four-step analysis and a weaker and a stronger sample paragraph side by side.

From Englisch Meistern

English writing workshop (Schreibwerkstatt)

Pick a text type and see a sample structure: analysis, discussion essay, creative writing or mediation. Plus PEEL, register and a 90-second final checklist.

From Englisch Meistern

English speaking exam (Kommunikationsprüfung)

Phrases as flashcards, timed scenarios for practising in pairs, typical examiner questions and a checklist for the weeks before the exam.

From Englisch Meistern: oral exams

French

French grammar (Französisch-Trainer)

Subjonctif, passé composé versus imparfait, si-clauses, pronoun order and the partitive article as flashcards, plus connectors and a quiz.

From Französisch Meistern

French text analysis (Textanalyse)

From résumer to discuter: task verbs by competence level, the figures de style and their effect, and a three-step analysis.

From Französisch Meistern

French writing workshop (Schreibwerkstatt)

Résumé, analysis, commentaire and création with sample structures, phrases for every essay part, the WO method for arguments and an exam timetable.

From Französisch Meistern

French speaking exam (Kommunikationsprüfung)

The three phases from monologue to examiner discussion, phrases from polite disagreement to compromise, timed scenarios and a checklist.

From Französisch Meistern

Learning and AI

Tools that help in any subject: planning, handling exam stress, taking part in class and using AI with intent.

Exam planner (Klausur-Planer)

Enter your exam date and topics and get a daily plan: knowledge check, summary sheets, active recall and a mock exam.

From Lernen meistern

Exam coach (Prüfungscoach)

Guided breathing exercises with a timer, a plan for blackouts and routines for the weeks before and the morning of the exam.

From Englisch Meistern and Lernen meistern

Class participation (Mitarbeit)

PREP for contributions in class, Cornell notes, a 15-minute preparation routine and how to structure a presentation, as flashcards and a quiz.

From Lernen meistern

Prompt workshop (Prompt-Werkstatt)

A builder that assembles your prompt from four questions, with a filled-in example, plus proven techniques and a routine for catching made-up answers.

From Künstliche Intelligenz Meistern

Choosing a degree

Two self-checks for the decision after school. They do not replace study counselling, but they show which way your answers lean.

Study compass (Studienkompass)

Computer science or information systems? Twelve situations show your tendency, and the result lists which answers pointed where.

From the upcoming study compass books

Dual study check (Duales-Studium-Check)

Dual or classic degree? Twelve situations on working style, resilience and freedom, with a result you can trace back.

From the upcoming book Duales Studium Meistern
