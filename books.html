Books

The first books grew out of study notes I collected while preparing for my own final exams in 2019: which methods earn marks in tests and which only cost time. They became the Meistern series on English, French and learning itself. The AI series followed in 2026 and is written for anyone who wants to use AI in everyday life or at work.

Learning and school

Study techniques for steady progress and step-by-step preparation for written and oral exams up to the Abitur.
