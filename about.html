About me

I am Pavisanth Pathmarajah. I write books on learning, languages and artificial intelligence, and I build the Daily Dose apps and the study tools on this website.

PaviLabs carries my first name. The motto Paving Paths to a smarter future describes what I care about: paths to knowledge people actually use in their daily lives.

How it started

In 2019 I finished my Abitur, the German university entrance exam, among the top graduates of my year with a grade average of 1.1. While preparing, I wrote down what earned marks in exams and what only cost time, and which routines held up when motivation faded.

Those notes became my first book, Englisch meistern, in the same year. Books on oral exams, French and learning itself followed.

The books

The Meistern series for German upper secondary school covers English, French and learning. The AI series came out in 2026 in German and English, from a first introduction without prior knowledge to a practitioner's handbook on prompt engineering, agents and automation. More books are in progress, among them one on choosing a degree and one on IT security.

Why the apps exist

A book explains things thoroughly, but it does not remind you to keep going. That is why the Daily Dose apps exist: one lesson of about five minutes a day, in four languages, with an exam and a certificate after each level. Daily Dose of Security is available on the App Store, and Daily Dose of AI has been submitted to Apple and Google for review.

The study tools

The fourteen exercises on this website let you try the methods from the books right away: flashcards, analysis and writing exercises, an exam planner, breathing exercises for exam stress, a prompt builder and two self-checks for choosing a degree. Everything runs in your browser without sign-up. The tools are in German.
