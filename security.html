How a dose works

You set a daily goal between 3 and 15 minutes. Every dose follows the same structure, and a daily streak keeps the habit going.

  1. Lesson

    A short text of about five minutes, built around a real case.

  2. Quiz

    A few questions, with every answer explained.

  3. Takeaway

    One concrete recommendation you can apply the same day.

Spear phishing, whaling and CEO fraud

Example from the lesson: The classic BEC pattern

“I’m in a confidential acquisition meeting and can’t take calls. I need you to process a payment to a new supplier today. Keep this between us until the deal is announced.”

Authority, urgency and enforced secrecy in three sentences. The secrecy exists to block exactly the verification that would kill the scam.

Quiz question

What does a typical CEO fraud (BEC) message ask for?Correct answer: An urgent, confidential wire transfer or gift card purchase

Takeaway

Treat any urgent, confidential payment request as fraud until you verify it outside the message itself.
