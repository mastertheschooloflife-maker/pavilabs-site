About me

My first books grew out of the study notes from my own exam preparation. Since then I write learning material that you can put to use straight away.

I finished my Abitur in 2019 with a grade average of 1.1, and the notes from that time became my first book the same year. Today I write about learning, languages and artificial intelligence, and I build the Daily Dose apps.

Where the content comes from

And what happens to your data.

Content

The exercises and apps build on the books. The app lessons refer to documented cases. Notes on exam formats follow published guidelines from individual German states, such as North Rhine-Westphalia and Hesse, and vary from state to state.

Data

The apps work without an account. Progress, streaks and certificates stay on your device, with no analytics software and no ad networks. Lesson videos from YouTube load only after your consent. This website serves its fonts from its own server.
