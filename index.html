Paving Paths to a smarter future

PaviLabs builds learning apps for two skills that everyday life increasingly demands: digital security and a confident, informed use of AI. Each lesson is short, grounded in a real case, and designed so that a few minutes a day add up to lasting knowledge.

One dose, three steps

Both apps work with a daily goal of 3 to 15 minutes. Every dose follows the same structure, and the daily streak keeps the habit going.

  1. Lesson

    A compact reading of about five minutes, built around a real case.

  2. Quiz

    A few focused questions, with every answer clearly explained.

  3. Takeaway

    One concrete recommendation you can apply the same day.

Privacy by principle

Both apps work entirely without an account. Progress, streaks and certificates are stored only on your device. The apps contain no analytics software and no ad networks. The only external content is the linked YouTube lesson videos, which load only after your explicit consent.
