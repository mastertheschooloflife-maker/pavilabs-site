One dose, three moves

You pick a daily goal between 3 and 15 minutes, and the app fills it one dose at a time. Streaks do the rest.

  1. Lesson

    About five minutes of reading, wrapped around one real case.

  2. Quiz

    A few questions, and every answer comes with its reasoning.

  3. Takeaway

    One thing to change today, small enough that you actually do it.

Private, because your data was never wanted

Both apps run without an account. Progress, streaks and certificates live in your phone's storage and nowhere else. There is no analytics SDK, no ad network, nothing phoning home. The only outside requests are the YouTube lesson videos, and they load after you allow them.
