Judging AI Output · Verification at work · 6 minutes

Verification workflows by stake level

Example from the lesson: One analyst, one afternoon, three tiers

An analyst uses a chatbot three times in an afternoon. First, brainstorming angles for a strategy workshop: Tier 0, she keeps what sparks and discards the rest unchecked. Second, summarizing an internal call recording for the team wiki: Tier 1, she spot-checks the two deadlines and one budget figure the summary contains, since those are what colleagues will act on.

Third, drafting sections of a client-facing market report: Tier 2, every market figure gets traced to its source, every cited study gets opened, and the document does not leave her desk until she can defend each sentence.

Quiz question

Why should stake classification happen at the start of a task rather than once the AI draft is finished?Correct answer: A finished, polished draft biases you toward leniency, while upfront classification sets the review depth before fluency can argue against it

Takeaway

Classify the stakes before you prompt, and let the tier decide the depth of your review.
